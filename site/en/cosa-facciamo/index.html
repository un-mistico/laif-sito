Where do we start?

Is LAIF a software house in Bologna? Do you also work in other cities?

Yes, LAIF is based in Bologna, at Via Galliera 32. We work alongside companies across Italy on software development, data and artificial intelligence, combining remote work with in-person sessions.

What does AI consulting for companies involve?

We start from a process and the data available. We assess feasibility and use cases, build a prototype and define how to check quality, limits and the need for oversight before integration.

Do I need a defined project already?

No. We can start from an operational problem and clarify priorities, users and constraints together before choosing the solution.

Can you work with the software we already use?

We assess the APIs, data and integration options of your existing systems, to understand what to keep and what to connect.

What happens after release?

The process includes adoption: training and hands-on support are part of the work. Support and evolution arrangements are defined within the project.
