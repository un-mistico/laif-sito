The problem.

The machine manuals were available digitally, but long and in several languages. Technicians often relied on colleagues’ experience and phone calls to find an answer.

The solution.

LAIF built an application where manuals can be uploaded and specific questions asked. The system searches the documentation and shows where the information is, through an interface that also works on phones and tablets.

Starting from the knowledge that already exists.

Working with Mattia Gerardi brought the focus onto the manuals and the work of the service team. The video also covers tailoring answers to the company’s safety guidelines and way of operating.
