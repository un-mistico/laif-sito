The problem.

As the clubs grew, manual processes became hard for the central team to sustain. Activities needed organising without giving up the existing booking platform.

The solution.

The work started with internal administration and data collection, then extended to the CRM. Integration with Playtomic made it possible to keep the booking system and connect it to the network’s operational needs.

Integrate before replacing.

The technical constraint was to work with a platform already used every day. In the video Guido Giardini talks about the API work and the shift from administrative needs to customer relationships.
