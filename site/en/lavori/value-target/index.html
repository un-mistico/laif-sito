Subsidised finance: data, artificial intelligence and an algorithm for reporting hours on funding calls.

Source
Il Salotto di LAIF

The problem.

When managing a funding call, one of the most complex aspects is reporting the hours employees have worked on projects: a process many companies are not set up to handle accurately and in compliance. In a sector made of red tape, deadlines and large volumes of information, time is everything.

The solution.

An algorithm that automatically cross-checks the data and validates the hours that are actually eligible. Alongside it, access to AI platforms for all technical staff and for sales too: those who know how to ask the right questions get better analyses and waste less time.

AI is not a fad.

On Il Salotto di LAIF, Francesco Raimondi, CEO of Value Target, explains how subsidised finance supports digitalisation, AI, sustainability and internationalisation, and the difference between innovation and research.
