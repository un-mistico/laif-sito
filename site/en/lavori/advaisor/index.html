The problem.

Comparing policy cover and conditions means reading long, technical documents. For an adviser, finding a detail while talking to a client is an everyday need.

The solution.

With Advaisor, LAIF worked on a custom AI solution to query insurance products and compare their features. The system’s language and behaviour were refined together with the people who use it.

An assistant, with supervision.

Fabio talks about the work on real use cases: which questions to ask, which details to compare and how to make answers relevant to the sector. AI supports the professional; the answers require their review.
