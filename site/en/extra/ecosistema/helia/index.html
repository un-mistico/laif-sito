Helia.
Voice AI that acts.

The AI voice agent born at LAIF for business customer care: it answers, organises knowledge and hands over to the team only when needed.

From a need,
an idea takes shape.

Calls and messages arrive at all hours. Many ask for information the company already has: the challenge is answering well, straight away, and keeping track of everything.

The project.

Helia, part of the LAIF group, handles customer requests across several channels: phone, WhatsApp and email. For each company it builds a knowledge base that grows with every interaction, and it integrates with CRM, ERP and calendar to book appointments and update systems.

  • AI switchboard, phone triage and customer care
  • Handover to the team with the full context of the conversation
  • Integration with the business systems already in use
