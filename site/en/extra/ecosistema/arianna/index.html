Arianna.
Finding your way when you care for someone.

The digital guide that supports caregivers and families in the day-to-day management of care.

From a need,
an idea takes shape.

People caring for someone often have to find their way through different needs and scattered information. Making the first step easier matters as much as the amount of content available.

The project.

Arianna is the digital platform for caregivers and families. You ask a question in plain words, get information based on selected sources, and find services, rights and support near you.

  • A digital assistant to help you find your way in care
  • Rights and protections, local services and support
  • Daily organisation and mental wellbeing

Visit the Arianna website ↗

The project, told together.

Artificial intelligence processes content from a multidisciplinary team of experts — legal, organisational, psychological and wellbeing — to offer personalised services to caregivers and patients. Not to replace human care, but to enhance it.
