Vivilo.
The right moments, to the right people.

Personalised photos, videos and reels for every participant at an event, in under 12 hours.

From a need,
an idea takes shape.

An event produces a huge amount of content. For participants, finding their own can be the hardest part.

The project.

Proprietary technology recognises each participant, finds the content they appear in and makes it available on a platform branded for the event. Vivilo can handle production with its own operators or work with the media team already on site.

  • Personalised photos, videos and reels for every participant
  • Content delivered in under 12 hours
  • Designed for marathons, races and sports events
