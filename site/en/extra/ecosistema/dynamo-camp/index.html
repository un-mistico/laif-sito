Dynamo Camp.
The right to happiness.

Free recreational therapy programmes for children and young people with serious or chronic illnesses, neurodevelopmental disorders or disabilities, and for their families.

The mission.

At Dynamo Camp, fun is therapy: it restores self-confidence and improves the quality of life of those who need it most.

The Camp.

A nature oasis in the province of Pistoia, plus City Camps in some of Italy’s main cities: a stimulating, safe environment in which to socialise, experiment, have fun and face life without barriers. Dynamo Camp is a member of SeriousFun Children’s Network, the international network founded in 1988 by Paul Newman.

  • Free recreational therapy for children and young people
  • Welcoming families, brothers and sisters too
  • Activities at the Camp in Tuscany and in cities
