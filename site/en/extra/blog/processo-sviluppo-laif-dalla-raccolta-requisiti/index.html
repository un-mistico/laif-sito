The Development Process at Laif: From Requirements Gathering to Customisation

Francesco Barbanti, Data & AI Engineer at LAIF, talked about it. Every project starts by gathering the client’s requirements, from core needs to desired improvements. Once the design is approved, we move on to development, split into backend and frontend, using AI engines to save time and respond quickly to needs.

Each version is released to the client to gather continuous feedback, keeping us closely aligned with expectations.

Customisation is our strength: tailored solutions for every client.
