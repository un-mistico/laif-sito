Two days with the LAIF and Helia team.

With Giuliano Formato, Director Data at ShopFully, on how to build a Data and AI team.

From LAIF’s LinkedIn page.

Two intense days in Bologna with the whole Laif and Helia team, devoted to discussion and to the state of the art of our work on AI 💡

We had the pleasure of hosting Giuliano Formato, Director Data at Shopfully, who told us how to build, scale and manage a Data and AI team. A down-to-earth conversation about the challenges, the rewards and the impact of a role like that. Hearing from someone who lives this world every day makes a huge difference.

Then we dived into Helia: its architecture, how it works, the technical choices that brought it to where it is today, and the challenges still open.

Finally, the Laif Agent: after delivering many agentic projects, it was time to pool our experience, standardise our approaches and stop reinventing the wheel every time. We talked about the challenges of this technology but above all about the concrete impact it is having on our clients, making their everyday work simpler.

As always, a moment to grow together! As people even before as professionals.
