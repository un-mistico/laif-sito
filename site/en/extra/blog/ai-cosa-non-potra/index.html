“I find myself thinking more about what AI won’t be able to do.”

Alessandro Grotti, full stack AI engineer: AI is a tool, and behind it you need someone who understands the problem.

From LAIF’s LinkedIn page.

Alessandro Grotti is a full stack AI engineer at Laif. He follows projects from start to finish with one fixed goal: finding the best solution for clients. That solution isn’t always AI, and he knows it better than anyone.

His view on AI is clear-cut: it is a tool. Powerful, useful, but there must always be someone behind it who understands the problem and knows where it makes sense to use it.

And the future? Alessandro prefers to think about what AI will never be able to do. Like enjoying a beautiful mountain view.
