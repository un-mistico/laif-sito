From LAIF’s LinkedIn page.

Andrea Mordenti, co-founder of Laif, replies to Federico Nicosia of NIVI SPA and touches on a point few are addressing openly: large generative models have almost run out of real data to train on.

The solution? Training them on synthetic data, produced by other AI models. A mechanism that puts concrete limits on the quality and scalability of these tools in the long run.

This changes how we should look at AI today: not as a technology growing endlessly, but as a tool to integrate intelligently, knowing what it can really do and what it can’t.
