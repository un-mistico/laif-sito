Laif looks beyond Italy’s borders.

Thanks to the 2024 call for internationalisation projects promoted by the Emilia-Romagna region, we launched the S.C.A.L.E. project, a structured programme to bring our solutions to the US market.

It wasn’t a leap in the dark. We built an offering based on scalable SaaS solutions, adaptable to local needs, and defined a marketing plan and a go-to-market approach designed to generate qualified B2B relationships and strengthen our international presence. One step at a time, with method. Because growing outside Italy takes the same care we put into building our products: start from the problem, understand the context, build something that really works. The journey has only just begun.

Project co-funded by the European Regional Development Fund. Action 1.3.2 PR-FESR Emilia-Romagna 2021-2027.
