Marco Pinelli, co-founder: three operational teams and the T-shape principle.

From LAIF’s LinkedIn page.

Growing means learning to get better organised.

Marco Pinelli, co-founder of Laif, describes how the company has evolved over time: from a place where everyone did a bit of everything to three dedicated operational teams, each with its own team leader and specialisms.

The principle behind this growth is the T-shape: deep expertise in your own area, without ever losing sight of the bigger picture around the final product.

"We’re no longer a fledgling startup. We’re an established company with a sustainable business model."
