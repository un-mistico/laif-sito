How LAIF began.

Bringing digital innovation to small and medium-sized businesses too.

From LAIF’s LinkedIn page.

How did LAIF begin? It all started from an obvious need: digital innovation projects are often reserved for large companies.

LAIF aims to change this paradigm.

The idea is to turn expertise in technology, software and data analysis into concrete solutions, designed specifically for small and medium-sized businesses.

Who better than Marco to lead this vision? LAIF is not just a name, but a symbol and an idea dedicated to those who truly want to innovate.
