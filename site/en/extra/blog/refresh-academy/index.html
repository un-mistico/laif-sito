Refresh Academy: technology that includes.

A reskilling programme to train new programmers, together with Refresh Academy.

From Marco Vita’s LinkedIn profile and the LAIF YouTube channel.

Technology that includes, not excludes. The collaboration between LAIF and Refresh Academy brings technical skills and social impact together.

The aim is to offer a full-time reskilling programme to people left out of the job market, training new programmers ready to start again with a digital qualification.

“Thank you Andrea Scavolini for involving Laif in the Refresh Academy project and for the lovely story!” — Marco Vita
