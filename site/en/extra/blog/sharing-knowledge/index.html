Sharing Knowledge.

Moments when we stop to tell each other what we're building.

From LAIF’s LinkedIn page.

At Laif we believe growth comes not only from projects, but also from sharing experiences.

That’s why we created Sharing Knowledge: moments when we step away from the daily rush to tell each other what we’re building. Best practices, successful projects, processes and ideas worth sharing with people who weren’t directly involved.

The goal is simple: make sure knowledge doesn’t stay locked inside one team, but is shared.

We’ve also decided to enrich these sessions by inviting outside guests, to bring new perspectives on cross-cutting topics. The first session was on personal finance and financial planning, with Sandro Vita , a professional with over 40 years’ experience in the field.

A moment of exchange that goes beyond work and helps the whole team grow! 💪
