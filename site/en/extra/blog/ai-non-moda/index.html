AI is not a fad. It’s time won back.

Francesco Raimondi, Value Target: AI for all the technical staff and the sales team.

From LAIF’s LinkedIn page.

AI is not a fad. It's time won back.

Francesco Raimondi, CEO of Value Target Srl - Finanza Agevolata, has given all his technical staff, and the sales team too, access to AI platforms. Not to follow a trend, but because those who know how to ask the right questions get better analysis, work better and waste less time.

In a sector like subsidised finance, made of bureaucracy, deadlines and huge volumes of information, time is everything. And AI, used with judgement, gives it back.
